## Decision

- **The slogan.** "The first agentic design system."
- **The line under it.** "Made for AI products, and for the agents that build them."
- **One slogan per surface.** The showcase hero, the README, the package description and the page title carry it; nothing else competes.
- **The triad stays.** Animus, anima and vita still explain the name; the slogan explains the purpose.

## Why

- **Purpose over poetry.** "Give your ideas life" was true of any design system; this line says who Vita is for: AI products, built by people and agents together.
- **Agentic is the difference.** Vita's decisions, skills and audit let an agent design like a designer; the slogan leads with that.

## Rejected

- **"The AI-agent-first design system".** Accurate, but it reads as a feature list, not a promise.

## Revisit when

- **The claim stops being true.** "First" is a claim: if it can't be defended, keep "The agentic design system" and drop "first".
