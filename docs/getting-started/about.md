## Mind, soul, body

- **Animus — the mind.** The backend logic that reasons and decides.
- **Anima — the soul.** The product's purpose, character and the human experience it creates.
- **Corpus — the body.** The design system: the form people touch, read and trust.

> [!NOTE] A mind without a body can't act, and a soul without a body can't be seen. Corpus gives every product one consistent shape, however complex the logic behind it.

## Two meanings

- **The product's body.** The visible form of every screen.
- **A body of work.** A shared collection of components, tokens and patterns that grows over time.

## What that means in practice

- **One body per product.** Every screen is built from Corpus. A local component is a limb grown outside the body, allowed only with a designer's approval.
- **The soul shapes the body.** Personas, purpose and voice decide the words and flows, through the taxonomy and the content skills.
- **The mind stays behind the body.** However complex the logic, users meet one calm, consistent surface.
- **The body of work grows.** Every gap becomes a proposal to Corpus, then flows back to every product with `corpus update`.

## How this site is organised

- **Foundations.** The anatomy: grid, spacing, color, type, motion, icons, accessibility and content.
- **Components.** The organs: what each is for, when to pick it, and when not to.
- **Patterns.** The gestures: how components combine to do a job end to end.

> [!TIP] Every page has the same four tabs: **Overview** (see it live), **Guidelines** (the rules), **Tokens** (what it's made of) and **Code** (how to use it).
