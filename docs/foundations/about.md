Every product we build has three parts, named after the classical view of a person as mind, soul and body.

| Part | Is the | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Animus** | the mind | The backend logic that reasons and decides. |
| **Anima** | the soul | The product's purpose, character and the human experience it creates. |
| **Corpus** | the body | The design system: the visible form through which the mind and soul reach the world. |

A mind without a body can't act, and a soul without a body can't be seen. Corpus is where the intelligence becomes something people can touch, read and trust. It gives the product one consistent shape, however complex the logic behind it.

Corpus also means **a body of work**: a shared collection of components, tokens and patterns that grows over time. The design system is both the product's body and the team's body of knowledge.

## What that means in practice

- **One body per product.** Every screen is built from Corpus foundations, components and patterns. A local component is a limb grown outside the body. It is not allowed, unless a designer explicitly approves it, and then it grows back into Corpus.
- **The soul shapes the body.** Anima (personas, purpose, voice) decides the words and the flows. That's why content runs through the taxonomy and the `corpus-personas` and `corpus-content` skills.
- **The mind stays behind the body.** However complex Animus becomes, the user meets one calm, consistent surface. Complexity is never an excuse for a new pattern.
- **The body of work grows.** Every gap found in a product becomes a proposal to Corpus. Components, tokens and patterns are added centrally, documented, and then flow back to every product with `corpus update`.

## How this site is organised

- **Foundations:** the anatomy (grid, spacing, color, typography, motion, icons, pictograms, accessibility, content).
- **Components:** the organs. What each one is for, when to choose it, and when not to.
- **Patterns:** the gestures. How components combine to do a job end to end.

Every page has the same four tabs:

1. **Overview:** see it live, plus when to use it and when not to.
2. **Guidelines:** the rules.
3. **Tokens:** exactly which tokens it's made of, resolved live.
4. **Code:** how to import it, and its source.
