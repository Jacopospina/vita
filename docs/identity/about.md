## The triad

- **Animus: the mind.** The backend logic. Deterministic code, data models, business rules and routing: structure, order and computational power.
- **Anima: the soul.** The AI layer. The language model that perceives, interprets context and speaks: intuition, voice and creative synthesis.
- **Vita: the life.** The design system. The visible, living experience where the mind's logic and the soul's intelligence become one thing people can touch, read and trust.

> [!NOTE] A mind without life can't act, and a soul without life can't be seen. Vita is where both meet the people who use them.

| | Role in a product | In Latin | What it gives |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Animus** | Backend logic and engine | The rational mind | Structure, rules, computation |
| **Anima** | The AI and language layer | The soul, the breath | Perception, language, synthesis |
| **Vita** | The design system and experience | Life, the living whole | One unified, visible experience |

## Why Vita

- **Life, not matter.** A design system is not a static collection of parts. It is an ecosystem that grows, moves and answers.
- **The sum, not a piece.** Vita is everything working together: the logic below, the intelligence beside it, and the experience in front.
- **Alive on screen.** Motion that never snaps, a thinking orb that breathes, words that awaken the maker: life is a quality you can see.

## Sofia, the anima made visible

- **The AI's presence.** Sofia is the thinking orb. Whenever the anima works (retrieving, generating, searching, listening, talking), Sofia shows it.
- **Never a spinner.** A spinner says "wait". Sofia says what the AI is doing, so people always know the soul of the product is at work.

## What that means in practice

- **One life per product.** Every screen is built from Vita. A local component grows outside the system, and needs a designer's approval.
- **The soul shapes the life.** Personas, purpose and voice decide the words and flows, through the taxonomy and the content skills.
- **The mind stays out of sight.** However complex the logic, people meet one calm, consistent surface.
- **The system keeps growing.** Every gap becomes a proposal to Vita, then flows back to every product with `vita update`.
